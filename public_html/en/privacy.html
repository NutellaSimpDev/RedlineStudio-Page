The personal data provided will be kept for the time necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected and to determine any possible liabilities that could derive from that purpose and the processing of the data.

5. DATA RECIPIENTS

REDLINE STUDIO will not transfer your personal data to third parties, except under express legal obligation or when strictly necessary for the provision of the requested service (for example, server infrastructure providers or analytical services in compliance with the GDPR).

6. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether we are processing your personal data. You can exercise your rights of:

  • Access: Right to know what data we are processing about you.
  • Rectification: Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Right to be forgotten): Right to request the deletion of your data when they are no longer necessary.
  • Restriction of processing: Right to request the temporary suspension of processing.
  • Objection: Right to object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.

To exercise these rights, you may send a written communication accompanied by a copy of your identity document to the email: contacto@redlinestudio.es.
